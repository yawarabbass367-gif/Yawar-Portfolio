# 1. Product Vision

Create a premium portfolio website that positions **{{FULL_NAME}}** as a **Product Designer** who solves complex business problems through research, strategy, UX, and product thinking.

The website should communicate professionalism, strategic thinking, and the ability to design enterprise-grade digital products rather than simply showcasing attractive UI.

---

# 2. Primary Goal

The website should convince visitors that the designer can successfully design products for:

- Banks
- Fintech Companies
- Enterprise Organizations
- SaaS Companies
- AI Products
- Startups

The portfolio should encourage visitors to:

- Contact me
- Schedule an interview
- Hire me
- Discuss freelance projects
- Offer product design opportunities

---

# 3. Target Audience

### Primary Audience

- Product Managers
- Startup Founders
- CTOs
- Recruiters
- Hiring Managers

### Secondary Audience

- Banks
- Fintech Companies
- Design Agencies
- Venture-backed Startups
- Enterprise Businesses

# 4. Brand Positioning

### Title

**Product Designer**

### Specialization

**Fintech • AI • Enterprise Platforms**

### Brand Statement

> I design digital products that simplify complex business workflows into intuitive, scalable, and user-centered experiences.

# 5. Core Brand Values

The portfolio should consistently communicate:

- Product Thinking
- Business Strategy
- User-Centered Design
- Simplicity
- Scalability
- Trust
- Innovation
- Professionalism
- Enterprise Experience

---

# 6. Design Personality

The overall experience should feel:

- Premium
- Minimal
- Modern
- Confident
- Elegant
- Intelligent
- Clean
- Purposeful

Avoid anything that feels overly decorative or trendy.

---

# 7. Design Inspiration

The visual direction should be inspired by the quality and clarity of products like:

- Apple
- Linear
- Stripe
- Figma
- Notion
- Vercel

Not copied—just inspired by their focus on clarity, typography, whitespace, and refinement.

# 10. Homepage Goals

The homepage should answer three questions within the first few seconds:

### Who are you?

Product Designer

### What do you design?

Enterprise software, fintech platforms, and AI-powered digital products.

### Why should I trust you?

Because you solve business problems through strategic product design and research-driven decision making.

---

# 11. Information Architecture

## Home

**Purpose:** Introduce the designer and build immediate credibility.

Suggested sections:

- Hero
- Selected Companies / Industries
- Featured Case Studies
- Design Philosophy
- Process Overview
- Featured Projects
- Call to Action

## About

**Purpose:** Tell the story behind the designer.

Suggested content:

- Professional Introduction
- Design Philosophy
- Product Design Approach
- Experience Timeline
- Skills
- Tools
- Industries Worked In

## Case Studies

**Purpose:** Show deep product thinking.

Each case study should include:

- Overview
- Problem
- Business Goals
- Users
- Research
- Challenges
- Process
- User Flows
- Wireframes
- Design System
- Final UI
- Results
- Lessons Learned

Focus on the thinking behind the decisions, not just the visuals.

Use: [templates/CASE_STUDY_TEMPLATE.md](./templates/CASE_STUDY_TEMPLATE.md).

## Projects

**Purpose:** Display additional work that may not require full case studies.

Each project should include:

- Thumbnail
- Project Name
- Industry
- Platform
- Role
- Brief Description
- Technologies (if applicable)

# 12. Key Messages

Visitors should leave the site believing:

- This designer understands products, not just screens.
- They can simplify complex enterprise workflows.
- They have experience with fintech and AI.
- They think strategically.
- They care about users and business outcomes.

---

# 13. Visual Language

- Minimal color palette
- Strong typography
- Large whitespace
- Clean grids
- Consistent spacing
- High-quality imagery and mockups
- Subtle, purposeful motion
- No unnecessary visual effects

---

# 14. Success Metrics

The portfolio should:

- Clearly communicate expertise within the first few seconds.
- Encourage visitors to explore case studies.
- Demonstrate a structured design process.
- Build credibility through professional presentation.
- Drive inquiries for interviews, collaborations, and client work.

---

# 15. Technical Goals

- Fully responsive across devices.
- Fast loading and optimized performance.
- Accessible (WCAG AA where practical).
- SEO-friendly.
- Component-based architecture.
- Easily maintainable and scalable.
- Smooth, lightweight animations that enhance the experience without distracting from the content.
